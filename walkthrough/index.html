Stop 1 of 3

See the data

For over a century, the national soil survey has sent scientists into the field to record what is underfoot, county by county. The result is 967,000 records across 4,089 survey areas, covering every county in the US.

It is one of the largest scientific datasets the public has ever paid for, and almost nobody can read it. We make it readable.

The Soil Atlas turns that archive into a national map with 7 story modes. Open it, pick a mode, and watch the country redraw itself by what is under the surface.
